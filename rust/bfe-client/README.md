# Native battle client

The native executable has one winit window/context host for version-2 battle
sessions and prepared map inspection. See [Native battle client](../../docs/native-client.md)
for recording, content and device options. Legacy near captures use the separate
headless `bfe-native` tool; the client rejects `calls.json` and version-1 sessions.

Audio runs the existing Rust master chain through cpal. `--pcm FILE` supplies
looping interleaved stereo f32 little-endian PCM at 48000 Hz. Without `--pcm` or
`--sound`, the same chain processes silence. The device must
support stereo at 48000 Hz; startup and stream errors stop the client.

`host::World` accepts generated command, look and flight records at 60 Hz,
then publishes retained renderer input from `present(alpha)`. The window host
bounds catch-up and keeps authority ticks separate from visual interpolation.
`RecordedWorld` publishes retained operation spans; a live native world can
replace that frame producer without changing the window/device host. `bfe-content-native::Loader` is the prepared-content admission boundary;
the earlier `content_loader` trait remains available to a future live host. No simulation
singleton, native pointer or per-entity callback crosses this boundary.

Input defaults follow `input.js::Controls` and `flight/controls.js`. Movement,
fire, actions and raw relative look use types generated by `tools/sim-schema.mjs`
from the existing client schema. View/map/stats and squad intentions are bounded
host values for a future native menu/world consumer. Captured camera matrices
remain recorded inputs; mouse movement does not alter the replay camera.

Full battle sessions use `bfe-client SESSION_DIR CONTENT_DIR`. The archive's
`session.json` and `blobs.bin` carry the browser's recorded world stream and
resource intentions. Native content supplies geometry, decoded textures,
collision trees, unit clips and effect definitions through `bfe-content-native`.
Native presentation recomputes output banks before the existing Rust passes
draw world, far, terrain, foliage, water, actors, effects, post and HUD. The
recorder verifies that the requested preset was applied. These sessions exit
after their last recorded frame; their camera remains recorded.

`--sound SOUND_DIR` attaches a sound archive to a full session. Admission runs
the retained Rust SoundControl owner from the same generated import, frame and
ready records used by the browser. Its computed commands, placements and room
updates drive `audio::output::Output`, including Master, through cpal. Samples
are admitted before device startup. Partial device quanta retain every sample;
commands and forwarding storage are reserved before the first callback.
`native-sound SOUND_DIR [--device]` checks native control banks and final PCM
against the production WASM host and optionally opens the audio device.
`tools/native-sound/capture.mjs OUTPUT [SCENE]` records the synthetic production
host fixtures, including pause, voice stealing, room queries and cached speech.
The current sound archive supports stereo speakers and imported analytic
collision planes. A battle recording with a live collision-world handle is
rejected until its native World collision owner is supplied. Streaming decode
credits, headphone filters and live command transport remain separate joins.

`bfe-client --map MAP CONTENT_DIR` admits prepared map content directly, opens
the same native window/context and lets WASD plus relative mouse move a free
camera. Space rises; Escape releases capture. `--seconds 20` prints initial and
final frame hashes and draw/admission counts. This inspector applies map lighting and fog, and admits opaque world/terrain,
far geometry, sky, initial prop rigs, foliage and water through `battle_frame`.
Live scene policy awaits a native World. Sound archives can accompany this
view using `--sound SOUND_DIR` independently of its camera clock.
